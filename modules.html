History

The Department of Computer Science was established to provide students with strong theoretical and practical skills in computing. Over the years, the department has grown and now offers programmes that prepare students for careers in the information technology industry.

Vision

To be a leading department in computer science education, research and innovation.

Mission

To produce well-rounded graduates who are able to apply computing knowledge to solve real-world problems and contribute to the development of society.

Location

The Department of Computer Science is located on the main campus, in the Science Faculty building.

Steps to Register for a Programme

  1. Apply online through the university admissions portal
  2. Submit the required certified documents
  3. Wait for an admission letter from the university
  4. Pay the registration fee
  5. Register for modules with the department
